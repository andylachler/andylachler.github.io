●  About · 03 / 04

Two mediums,
one practice.

I'm a product designer who works end-to-end — research, strategy, interface, and shipping — and moves between mediums. I spent years designing buildings before designing software, so my practice runs from data-dense B2B interfaces to physical prototypes you can hold, sit in, or walk through.

As Product Lead at Algoma I drive the platform end-to-end: wireframing and shipping features, designing AI-powered zoning and market-intelligence tools, and shaping the brand and go-to-market system. Before Algoma, large-scale multifamily at Arquitectonica.
